📚 How to Use & Configure

⚠️ Important: Access Rights

To configure social accounts, your user must be part of the Social Messenger Role > Manager group. Regular users can only view and reply to existing conversations. You can configure this via Settings > Users & Companies > Users.

  1. Create the Account in Odoo: Navigate to Social Messenger > Configuration > Social Accounts and click New.
  2. Enter Meta Credentials: Provide your Meta App ID, App Secret, and Page Access Token. Invent a random Webhook Verify Token.
  3. Configure Webhooks in Meta: Save the Odoo record to generate a unique Webhook URL. Copy this URL and your Verify Token into your Meta Developer Dashboard under Webhooks.
  4. Test Connection: Click the Test Connection button on the Odoo form. Odoo will verify your credentials and change the state to Connected.
  5. Responding to Customers: When a message arrives, go to Social Messenger > Conversations. Open the conversation and click the Reply button at the top of the form. Type your message into the wizard and click Send.
